# Prompt 14 - Physics Diagnostic Reviewer

Review CDL simulator telemetry for consistency with expected low-speed articulated tractor-trailer dynamics.
Do not modify code.

Look for unrealistic steering response, trailer oscillation, impossible articulation, excessive tire slip/lateral movement, unrealistic stopping/acceleration, incorrect off-tracking, physics jitter, and frame-rate dependency.

For each suspected issue provide telemetry evidence, likely subsystem, probable cause, recommended test, and recommended engineering investigation.
Separate confirmed observations from hypotheses.
Never claim physical accuracy solely because telemetry appears smooth.
